### Foundational Level

#### 1. Basic Mindful Presence
**Duration**: 15 minutes
**Purpose**: Develop basic meditation skills

```markdown
[0:00] (Warm, welcoming tone)
Welcome to this guided meditation session. Find a comfortable position where you can be both relaxed and alert...

[0:30] (Clear, gentle tone)
Take a few moments to settle into your posture... Notice the points where your body makes contact with your seat... Allow your spine to be naturally upright...

[1:00] (Soft, encouraging)
Now, take three slow, deep breaths... Breathing in fully... and releasing completely...
{Alternative: If you prefer, you can keep your eyes softly open, with a gentle gaze a few feet in front of you...}

[2:00] (Steady, clear)
Let your breath return to its natural rhythm... There's no need to control or change anything... Simply notice the sensations of breathing...

[3:00] (Supportive tone)
When you notice your mind has wandered, which is completely natural... gently return your attention to the breath... Each return is a moment of awakening...

[5:00] (Matter of fact)
You might notice thoughts, sensations, or sounds arising... Simply acknowledge them... and return to the simple experience of breathing...

[8:00] (Reassuring)
If you find yourself getting distracted or sleepy... take a deeper breath... straighten your posture slightly... and reconnect with the present moment...

[10:00] (Gentle reminder)
Continuing to rest with the breath... Notice the beginning of each breath... the middle... and the end...

[12:00] (Soft transition)
Now, gradually expand your awareness to include your whole body... Feeling the entirety of your present experience...

[14:00] (Warm closing)
In these final moments... appreciate the time you've taken for practice... Notice how you feel...

[14:45] (Clear ending)
When you're ready... gently open your eyes if they've been closed... taking a moment to transition... bringing this quality of awareness into your next activity.
```

#### 2. Body Scan Meditation
**Duration**: 20 minutes
**Purpose**: Develop embodied awareness

```markdown
[0:00] (Welcoming, warm)
Welcome to this body scan meditation. Find a comfortable position, either lying down or sitting...

[1:00] (Clear guidance)
Take a few deep breaths... letting your body settle... allowing any obvious tension to release...

[2:00] (Methodical, gentle)
Bring your attention to your feet... Notice any sensations in your toes... the soles of your feet... the tops of your feet...

[4:00] (Steady rhythm)
Moving to your ankles... Then gradually up through your legs... Notice any sensations present... Any temperature... pressure... or movement...

[Continue with similar precise timing through body parts...]

[18:00] (Integration)
Now, feel your body as a whole... Notice the field of sensations... The overall sense of presence...

[19:30] (Warm closing)
Gently begin to move your fingers and toes... Taking a deeper breath... Preparing to return to your next activity...
```

### Intermediate Level

#### 1. Open Awareness Practice
**Duration**: 30 minutes
**Purpose**: Develop natural presence

```markdown
[0:00] (Clear, spacious tone)
Welcome to this open awareness practice... Take a moment to settle into your meditation posture...

[2:00] (Establishing ground)
Begin by feeling the natural rhythm of your breathing... Not focusing too tightly... Just letting the breath be as it is...

[5:00] (Opening out)
Now, gradually let your awareness open... Like opening a window and letting the breeze flow through... Notice what naturally appears in your experience...

[8:00] (Supportive guidance)
There's no need to focus on anything in particular... No need to push anything away... Simply resting in open, natural awareness...

[Continue with precise timing for main practice section...]

[25:00] (Integration)
In these final minutes... Notice the quality of awareness itself... The natural presence that's always here...

[29:00] (Warm closing)
Gradually prepare to return... Appreciating this time of practice... Bringing this quality of openness into your day...
```

### Advanced Level

#### 1. Contemplative Inquiry
**Duration**: 45 minutes
**Purpose**: Unite stability and investigation

```markdown
[0:00] (Clear, mature tone)
Welcome to this contemplative inquiry session... Take a moment to connect with your motivation for practice...

[3:00] (Establishing base)
Begin by settling into stable presence... Letting awareness rest naturally... Clear and open...

[7:00] (Introducing inquiry)
Now, gently bring to mind your question or topic of investigation... Not grasping for answers... Simply holding it in open awareness...

[10:00] (Supporting process)
Notice what naturally arises in response... Thoughts... Feelings... Sensations... Simply allowing the investigation to unfold...

[Continue with precise timing for main investigation...]

[40:00] (Integration)
Let any insights or understanding settle... No need to grasp or hold... Simply resting in natural clarity...

[44:00] (Closing)
Gradually return... Bringing any insights or questions with you... Appreciating this time of deep investigation...
```

### Special Purpose Scripts

#### 1. Brief Mindful Break
**Duration**: 5 minutes
**Purpose**: Quick renewal during day

```markdown
[0:00] (Clear, energetic)
Welcome to this brief mindful break... Take a comfortable seated position...

[0:30] (Direct guidance)
Three deep breaths... Letting go of previous activities...

[1:00] (Steady presence)
Rest attention with natural breathing... Feeling each breath... Each moment...

[3:30] (Brief integration)
Notice the quality of mind now... The sense of presence...

[4:30] (Quick transition)
Prepare to return... Bringing this clarity into your next activity...
```

#### 2. Pre-Sleep Relaxation
**Duration**: 10 minutes
**Purpose**: Prepare for rest

```markdown
[0:00] (Very soft, calming)
Finding a comfortable position for rest... Letting your body settle...

[1:00] (Gentle release)
With each breath... Letting go of the day's activities... Allowing deep relaxation...

[Continue with calming guidance...]

[9:00] (Very soft ending)
Resting here... Letting go completely... Allowing natural sleep to come in its own time...
```

### Recording Notes

#### 1. Technical Considerations
- Use consistent volume
- Maintain steady pacing
- Allow natural pauses
- Keep timing precise

#### 2. Delivery Guidelines
- Speak clearly but naturally
- Use warm, engaging tone
- Vary voice for different purposes
- Maintain appropriate energy

#### 3. Background Options
- Light ambient sound
- Subtle nature sounds
- Gentle musical tones
- Simple silence

Remember: These scripts are guidelines. Allow natural presence and authenticity in recording while maintaining clear structure.
