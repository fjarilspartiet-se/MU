# Practice Journaling Guide
## Documenting Your Journey of Understanding

### Introduction to Practice Journaling

#### Purpose of Practice Journaling
- Document your development
- Track insights and questions
- Notice patterns and progress
- Support integration of understanding
- Create foundation for reflection

#### Types of Journal Entries

1. **Daily Practice Log**
   ```markdown
   Date: [Date]
   Time: [Time]
   Duration: [Minutes]
   Type: [Meditation/Contemplation/Investigation]

   Experience Notes:
   - Quality of attention:
   - Notable moments:
   - Challenges:
   - Questions arising:

   Post-Practice Reflection:
   - Key insight:
   - Application potential:
   - Support needed:
   ```

2. **Investigation Entry**
   ```markdown
   Question/Topic: [Your starting point]

   Initial Understanding:
   - What I currently think/know:
   - What I'm unsure about:
   - What I want to understand:

   Investigation Process:
   - What I observed:
   - What changed:
   - New questions:

   Integration:
   - How this affects my understanding:
   - How this applies to life:
   - Next steps for investigation:
   ```

3. **Insight Documentation**
   ```markdown
   Insight Moment:
   - When it arose:
   - Context:
   - Clear description:

   Understanding:
   - What changed:
   - What opened up:
   - What questions emerged:

   Application:
   - How this affects my view:
   - How to apply this:
   - What to investigate next:
   ```

### Path-Specific Journal Prompts

#### Wonder Path Prompts
1. **Experience Focus**
   - What catches my attention today?
   - What's new or surprising?
   - What feels mysterious?
   - What questions arise naturally?

2. **Observation Development**
   - What details do I notice?
   - How does attention move?
   - What patterns emerge?
   - What changes do I notice?

3. **Integration Questions**
   - How does this relate to daily life?
   - What's changing in my understanding?
   - What feels important to explore?
   - What support would help?

#### Inquiry Path Prompts
1. **Investigation Focus**
   - What's the core question?
   - What methods am I using?
   - What evidence emerges?
   - What conclusions arise?

2. **Understanding Development**
   - How is understanding deepening?
   - What connections appear?
   - What assumptions are revealed?
   - What needs more investigation?

3. **Teaching Preparation**
   - How would I explain this?
   - What examples help?
   - What questions might arise?
   - What's most important?

#### Wisdom Path Prompts
1. **Integration Focus**
   - How does this transform understanding?
   - What embodies in action?
   - What serves others?
   - What emerges naturally?

2. **Service Orientation**
   - How does this benefit others?
   - What needs attention?
   - How to share effectively?
   - What support to offer?

3. **Leadership Development**
   - What guidance emerges?
   - How to create conditions?
   - What needs development?
   - What vision emerges?
