# Student Self-Assessment Guide
## Understanding Your Journey in MU

### Introduction
This guide helps you assess your development and readiness for transitions between paths. Remember, self-assessment is not about judgment but about understanding where you are and what might support your continued growth.

### Part 1: Daily Practice Review

#### Practice Journal Template
```markdown
Date: [Date]
Practice Duration: [Time]

1. Today's Experience:
   - What was clear or stable in my practice?
   - What challenges arose?
   - What insights or questions emerged?

2. Integration Notes:
   - How did insights connect to daily life?
   - What changed in my understanding?
   - What questions remain?

3. Next Steps:
   - What needs more investigation?
   - What support would help?
   - What am I curious about?
```

#### Weekly Review Questions
1. **Practice Development**
   - How regular was my practice this week?
   - What patterns do I notice in my attention?
   - What supports or hinders my practice?

2. **Understanding Growth**
   - What new insights emerged?
   - How is my understanding developing?
   - What questions are alive for me?

3. **Life Integration**
   - How do insights affect daily life?
   - What changes am I noticing?
   - Where do I need support?

### Part 2: Transition Readiness Self-Check

#### Wonder Path to Inquiry Path

**Practice Stability Check**
- [ ] I maintain regular daily practice
- [ ] I can stay with experience for 15+ minutes
- [ ] I notice subtle aspects of experience
- [ ] I handle basic challenges well
- [ ] Practice feels natural and important

**Question Development Check**
- [ ] Questions arise naturally from experience
- [ ] I'm curious about deeper investigation
- [ ] I see connections between different areas
- [ ] My questions lead to meaningful insights
- [ ] I enjoy exploring and investigating

**Integration Check**
- [ ] Insights affect my daily life
- [ ] I notice changes in my understanding
- [ ] Practice and life feel connected
- [ ] I share naturally with others
- [ ] I engage actively in the community

#### Inquiry Path to Wisdom Path

**Advanced Practice Check**
- [ ] Hour-long practice is comfortable
- [ ] Investigation feels natural
- [ ] I handle challenges skillfully
- [ ] Insights arise regularly
- [ ] Practice integrates with life

**Teaching Readiness Check**
- [ ] I can explain clearly to others
- [ ] I understand different perspectives
- [ ] I support others naturally
- [ ] Teaching feels authentic
- [ ] Others find my sharing helpful

**Service Orientation Check**
- [ ] I contribute to the community
- [ ] Supporting others feels natural
- [ ] I take initiative in helping
- [ ] I understand others' needs
- [ ] Service enriches my practice

### Part 5: Working with Challenges

#### Common Challenges and Solutions

1. **Practice Challenges**
   - Inconsistency → Start small, build gradually
   - Doubt → Connect with community support
   - Confusion → Seek mentor guidance

2. **Understanding Challenges**
   - Unclear concepts → Use study resources
   - Integration difficulty → Focus on application
   - Teaching blocks → Start with basics

3. **Community Challenges**
   - Participation hesitation → Start small
   - Communication difficulty → Practice openness
   - Support needs → Ask directly
